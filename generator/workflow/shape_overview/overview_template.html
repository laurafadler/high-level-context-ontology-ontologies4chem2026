Context requirement shapes

What the SHACL shapes require for each context level and category. Pick a cell to see its requirements, or scroll through all of them below.

How to read this page

Context object
The thing a requirement is about: a device, a phase or a molecule. Which of the three it is, is its context level (Device, Phase, Molecule).
Category
One of six kinds of context: Information, Energy, Time, Space, Structure, Substance. A context level and a category together form one cell of the matrix below (in the ontology a hlo:ContextAspect).
Levels
Everything is described on up to three levels: a library entry (hlo:Fixed) with what is always true of it, a series record and an experiment record. Each record points to the one above through hlo:specializationOf (the chain). In the ontology the levels are scopes (hlo:Scope). A template (hlo:Template) is a library entry for a kind of object, such as a catalogue item, rather than for one identifiable object.
In use
A series or experiment record, as opposed to a library entry. Most requirements apply only to things in use.
Actual
A quantity as an entity of its own, e.g. the temperature of a phase. Its value stands on an Evidence (measured, observed, calculated) or a Reference (set, planned, controlled, assumed, literature). An Actual may be empty: a requirement asks that the quantity is described, not that its value is known.
Crate
The RO-Crate that packages one experimental series with its experiments.
Severity
A missing piece of context is a Warning: the data stays valid, it is only less complete. Info marks paths that only collect; assignments never report.

Kinds of requirements

ExistenceAsks that something exists at the context object, e.g. a temperature at every phase in use. Reports a Warning if it is missing. What it finds belongs to the context.
SurroundingsCollects what surrounds the context object, e.g. the energy fields of the room. Never reports. What it finds is shown with the context but not part of it.
AssignmentAdds to the context what exists but cannot be asked for, e.g. the reactions of a phase. Never reports.
