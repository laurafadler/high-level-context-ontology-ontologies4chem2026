### cpo:Lid

> A lid, cap or cover that closes the opening of a vessel. A device of its own, not a second phase of the vessel's body: it is taken off and put back, and what passes through it (a stirrer shaft, a sparger, a sensor) touches the lid, not the vessel wall. Typical forms: a disc (cpo:SolidCylinderForm) for a flat lid, a ring (cpo:AnnulusForm) for a screw cap whose opening a septum closes.

### cpo:Pipe

> A line that carries a stream between two devices: a rigid pipe, a flexible tube or a hose - which one is said by its body material and dimensions, not by a class of its own. Typical Form: a cpo:TubeForm whose cavity the stream fills (cpo:materialContainedIn the pipe, rule 3a.9), so the stream reaches the surroundings and their fields through the pipe. Named after DEXPI's piping components.
